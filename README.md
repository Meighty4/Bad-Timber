# Bad Timber

### Inspiration


Inspired by:  [DUCK SHOOT](https://plays.org/game/duck-shoot/)

### Description
A shooting gallery with a dark twist, a game of hunting and being hunted.  
Shoot the targets to fill the ever dropping ~~bloo~~...fun-o-meter. 
 Um, just...watch out for the falling trees.

### Gameplay

Clicking on targets gives you a varying amount of points, the rabbits are worth 3, and deer 5 points each! The trees, though, are obstacles.  
Shooting them brings down the fun-o-meter faster and they spill out to the row above them, temporarily blocking the targets they pass in front of.
